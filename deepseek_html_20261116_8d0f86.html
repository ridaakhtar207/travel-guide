Karachi, briefly

Karachi is Pakistan’s sprawling port megacity on the Arabian Sea, a place where colonial-era architecture, bustling bazaars, and a legendary food scene collide. As the country’s commercial heart, it pulses with energy, drawing diverse communities and offering a raw, unfiltered taste of urban Pakistan. Beyond the traffic and heat lies a city of surprising charm, from serene beaches to grand marble monuments.

Three attractions worth your time

  • Mazar-e-Quaid

    The striking white-marble mausoleum of Pakistan’s founder, Muhammad Ali Jinnah, set in peaceful gardens. It’s a solemn, iconic landmark and a must-see for understanding the nation’s history.

  • Clifton Beach

    The city’s seaside escape along the Arabian Sea. While the water isn’t for swimming, it’s perfect for evening strolls, camel rides, and snacking on local street food while watching the sunset.

  • Mohatta Palace Museum

    Housed in an elegant pink-stone palace, this museum showcases art and cultural exhibits. The building itself, with its intricate architecture and lush gardens, is a photographer’s delight.

Local tip

For getting around safely and avoiding haggling in the heat, rely on ride-hailing apps like Careem or Uber rather than hailing random taxis or rickshaws. It’s affordable, air-conditioned, and saves you the stress of negotiating fares.
